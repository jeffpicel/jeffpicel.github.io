Jeffrey Picel

Independent AI researcher · AI safety, reasoning and evaluation

I study learned critics: small networks that read a language model's internal state partway through a task and forecast how the attempt will end. I use them to steer reasoning and to guard tool-using agents, for example against instructions planted in the data an agent reads (prompt injection), and I compare them with training the agent itself by reinforcement learning. A paper is in preparation.

Before this I spent about seven years as a data scientist and machine learning engineer: at Airbnb, then at Faire, where I was a founding data scientist and machine learning engineer for the brand side of the marketplace (Senior, promoted to Staff in 2023). There I led data science for marketplace quality, weighing the cost of missed harm against the cost of penalizing good brands, built ranking and causal-measurement systems in production, and co-authored the company's first experimentation guide. I have a PhD in economics from Harvard, and degrees in mathematics and economics from the University of Chicago.
